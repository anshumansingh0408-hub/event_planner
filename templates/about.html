About Utsav

Utsav is an independent event planning application for Indian celebrations, including weddings, birthdays, and community gatherings. It turns a few basic inputs into a complete, structured plan with realistic budget splits, countdown deadlines, and vendor negotiation checklists. Everything runs in your browser without requiring an account or collecting personal data.

The problem

Planning a major celebration usually means juggling money, dates, and vendor quotes across scattered spreadsheets, note apps, and endless WhatsApp chat threads. Key details get lost, budgets blow out unexpectedly, and organizers scramble as the event date draws closer. Utsav brings these separate threads together into a single, cohesive plan where numbers, countdowns, and checklists stay aligned.

How your plan is built

The deterministic rules engine handles all calculations; the AI only assists with creative ideas and questions.

Step 1

Your inputs

Event type, budget, guest count, celebration date, and priorities.

Step 2

Rules engine

Pure deterministic logic computes the budget split, timeline, and vendor checklist.

Step 3

AI ideas

Gemini writes creative theme, menu, and decor ideas on demand without touching numbers.

Step 4

Your plan

An actionable, printable celebration roadmap grounded in your real budget and timeline.

What the AI does, and what it doesn't

We maintain a strict boundary between quantitative planning logic and generative suggestions.

What the AI does

When requested, Gemini drafts creative celebration themes, catering menu possibilities, decor concepts, and attendee activities. It also answers plain-language questions by reading your computed plan summary.

What the AI does not do

The AI is never allowed to modify your budget figures, change calendar dates, invent vendor names, quote binding market prices, or book suppliers. If Gemini is unreachable, Utsav provides curated rule-based fallback ideas.

Privacy

Utsav requires no user accounts and stores no saved plans on server disks. Your plan parameters remain entirely within your browser session. When you click "Get ideas" or "Ask", a compact summary of your plan numbers along with your note or question is securely sent to Google's Gemini API over HTTPS. Recent AI responses are cached in server memory for up to 15 minutes to prevent redundant network calls, and are discarded thereafter.

Limits

  • All calculations and estimates are denominated in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹). Other currencies are not currently supported.
  • Interface and AI responses are currently provided in English only.
  • Market prices fluctuate significantly depending on city tier, season peak dates, and vendor reputation. Confirm formal quotes directly with vendors.
  • Utsav does not operate a vendor database, directory, or direct booking mechanism.
  • The application is a self-serve planning aid and is not a replacement for a professional on-site wedding planner or event director for complex celebrations.
