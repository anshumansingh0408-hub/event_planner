About Utsav

A practical planning tool for Indian celebrations and community gatherings.

What it does

Utsav takes your event type, total budget, guest count, and celebration date to build a concrete starting plan. It provides a percentage-based budget breakdown in whole Indian Rupees, a milestone countdown timeline, and categorized vendor checklists with interview questions.

How the numbers are calculated

Budget allocations are based on empirical planning assumptions across weddings, birthdays, and community gatherings. When you pick up to two priority categories, each receives a 5 percentage point increase funded proportionally from non-priority categories (excluding contingency), and rounding drift is balanced so amounts sum exactly to your total budget. The countdown timeline calculates milestone deadlines backwards from your event date. If an event is scheduled on short notice, overdue milestones are evenly rescheduled across the initial 40% of remaining days.

Where AI is used

Google Gemini AI is used exclusively in the Ideas and Ask tabs to suggest creative themes, menu concepts, and answer specific questions. The AI receives a compact summary of your computed plan and never alters the calculated budget or timeline numbers. If the AI service is unreachable or unconfigured, Utsav automatically provides comprehensive rule-based fallback ideas.

Limits

Actual vendor rates fluctuate significantly across different Indian cities, peak wedding dates, and festive seasons. All figures and checklists are structured estimates intended to guide your preparations. They do not constitute official vendor quotes and do not replace professional event coordination for high-complexity gatherings.
