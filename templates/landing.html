    What the AI does, and what it doesn't

    Utsav separates deterministic mathematics from creative generative suggestions.

    What the AI does

    • ✓ Suggests themes, menus, activities and decor tailored to your guest count and notes.
    • ✓ Answers specific questions grounded strictly in your calculated budget and dates.
    • ✓ Provides sensible discussion points for family or organizing committees.

    What the AI does not do

    • ✕ Never changes your budget allocations, guest counts, or scheduled dates.
    • ✕ Never names vendors, invents contact information, or quotes market rates.
    • ✕ Never books services or handles financial transactions.

    If the AI is unavailable, you still get the full plan and general ideas.

    Frequently asked questions

    Common questions about privacy, accuracy, and event planning with Utsav.

    Do I need an account? ▾

    No. Plan details stay in your browser.

    What is sent to the AI? ▾

    A summary of your plan plus your preferences or question, only when you click "Get ideas" or "Ask".

    How accurate are the amounts? ▾

    Splits are planning assumptions for each event type; prices vary by city and season, so confirm quotes with vendors.

    What if my event is soon? ▾

    Overdue tasks are rescheduled into the days you have left and labelled "Rescheduled".

    Can I use it outside India? ▾

    Amounts are in rupees; other currencies are not supported yet.
